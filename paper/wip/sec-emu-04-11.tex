\section{FP64 emulation on one laptop}\label{sec:emu}
FP64 from INT8 is not our claim, and two public implementations of it run on this laptop: cuBLAS~13's own emulation
(\texttt{CUBLAS\_COMPUTE\_64F\_EMULATED\_FIXEDPOINT}, cuBLAS 13.8.2 from NVIDIA's wheels, 8~GB workspace; its automatic dynamic
precision, ADP, or a fixed number of mantissa bits)~\cite{nvemu,cublasdoc} and GEMMul8~\cite{gemmul8} (commit 30b5b5d, INT8
backend, fast and accurate modes). We ran the three in one held window with one harness (\texttt{bench/emu\_harness.h},
\texttt{bench/fair\_jobs.sh}, 4 November 2026): the same inputs (normal values times $2^{u}$, $u$ uniform in $[-s,s]$), the error
componentwise against an exact-to-$2^{-80}$ reference at $M=N=256$, and the time as the median of five calls after a warm-up.
