Title:      Precision as a software choice: FP64, double-double, triple-double and beyond from the INT8 tensor cores of a laptop GPU
Authors:    Shaban Ejupi
Primary:    cs.MS (Mathematical Software)
Cross-list: cs.DC, cs.SC, math.NA
License:    arXiv.org perpetual, non-exclusive license (chosen 29-10-2026)
Comments:   20 pages. Code and every measurement: https://git.spacecode.tech/shaban/ozaki-blackwell

Abstract (plain text, under arXiv's 1920 characters):
Consumer and laptop GPUs run FP64 at 1/64 of their FP32 rate: on a laptop Blackwell GPU (RTX PRO 3000, sm_120) we measure
232 TOPS of INT8 mma.sync against 0.449 TFLOPS of native FP64 (517x). With the Ozaki scheme II we implement in CUDA C and PTX
matrix multiplication at FP64 (6.7 TFLOPS at n=4096, where cuBLAS 13's own emulation reaches 8.5), double-double (3.81
TFLOPS), triple-double (2.03 TFLOPS) and up to 424 bits from the same INT8 units, to our knowledge the first such GEMMs beyond
FP64 on GPU INT8 tensor cores. Solving Ax=b with condition number 1e20 in double-double takes 0.692 s at n=8192, 22x sooner
than the same LU blocked on 24 CPU threads and 629x sooner than __float128; at condition number 1e36, where double-double has
no correct digit, a triple-double LU is 9x faster than the same blocked CPU LU and 166x than MPFR. Exact integer matrix
products with 1,015- to 131,063-bit entries run 10-19x faster than FLINT on 24 CPU threads; exact determinants come up to
355x (int8, n=8192) and 404x (64-bit entries, n=4096) sooner than FLINT; on 15 of NIST Matrix Market's most ill-conditioned
real matrices the certified exact solution is 9-7,857x faster than SPEX where SPEX finished. Power-bound at 94-117 W, the
GPU reaches 91% of its sustained INT8 rate with Strassen over Z/m (in classical operations), and an FP64-accurate DGEMM costs
13.9x less energy than its native FP64, each at its most efficient clock. The same units give correctly rounded MXFP4, NVFP4,
FP8 and BF16 products: every output of Kimi K3's 48 expert matrices (2.78 trillion parameters) is certified, where BF16
tensor cores with FP32 accumulation round 51-83% of them differently; Nemotron 3 Ultra (560 billion parameters) runs from
disk with every linear map correctly rounded at 19.5-19.7 s per token, as fast as FP32 accumulation.
